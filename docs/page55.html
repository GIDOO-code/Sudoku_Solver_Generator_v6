GroupedNiceLoop

GroupedNiceLoop is a super link version of NiceLoop.
GroupedNiceLoop is a chain of Intta-cell link, Inter-cell link, AIC linkALS link. ALS link, ALS-XZ link.
Link continuity is the same as NiceLoop.

Examples 5 and 6 illustrate how contradictions can arise. A path starting from a particular cell can lead to both positive and negative outcomes for a specific cell or candidate number.
In this case, the starting cell and the digit is negated.
